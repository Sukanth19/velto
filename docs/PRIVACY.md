# Velto privacy policy

_Last updated: 30 October 2026_

Velto is a productivity app: tasks, focus sessions, habits, a quick-capture inbox and a mood journal. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into it.

## The short version

Everything you enter stays on your phone. Velto has no accounts, no servers, no analytics and no ads, and it doesn't send your data anywhere.

## What Velto stores, and where

Velto stores your tasks and their steps, focus sessions, habits and check-ins, inbox items, mood and energy check-ins (including any notes), and your settings. All of it is kept in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps can't read.

## Backups

- **Android backup.** If Android's backup is turned on for your device, your Velto data is included in your Google backup and when you move to a new phone, like other apps' data. This is controlled by your Android settings and handled by Google under its own privacy policy. Velto doesn't receive any of it.
- **Export.** You can export your data to a file from Settings. That file goes wherever you choose to save it, and you're responsible for where you keep or share it.

## Crash reports

If Velto crashes, details of the error (the technical error message, the app version and your phone model and Android version) are saved on your phone. The next time you open Velto it asks whether you want to send them. Nothing is sent unless you choose **Share** or **Report on GitHub**, and you can see the report before sending it. The report contains nothing you've written in the app.

## Permissions

- **Notifications**: to tell you when focus time and breaks end, and for the optional daily reminders and the weekly review.
- **Do Not Disturb access**: only if you turn on "Silence notifications while focusing". Velto uses it to turn Do Not Disturb on during focus and back off afterwards.

Velto doesn't use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or the internet.

## Sharing

The only things that leave your phone are things you share yourself: an exported backup file, a "week in review" image, or a crash report.

## Children

Velto doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.
