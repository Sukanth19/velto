Velto takes the deciding out of getting things done. Add your tasks, tap Just Start, and Velto picks the one that matters most right now and starts a focus timer. No scrolling through lists, no second-guessing.

<b>Decide less</b>
• Just Start: one tap picks your best task by urgency, importance, effort and energy, and starts focusing
• Can't decide? Say how long you've got and how much energy you have, and Velto spins up the best fit
• Due dates raise a task's urgency as the deadline gets close, so nothing sneaks up on you
• Priority matrix view sorts everything into Do first, Schedule, Quick win and Later

<b>Focus</b>
• A squiggly Material You timer that goes flat when you pause
• Break tasks into steps and see the next one while you work
• +5 minutes when you need just a bit more
• Silence notifications while you focus, and control the timer from the Quick Settings tile

<b>Build habits</b>
• One-tap daily check-ins with streaks and a 12-week activity grid
• Repeating tasks: daily, weekdays, weekly or monthly
• Gentle reminders, only when there's something left to do

<b>Understand yourself</b>
• Mood and energy check-ins, a year in pixels, and how your energy affects your focus
• Week in review: wins, trends against last week and one thing to try next, shareable as an image
• Learn the time of day you focus best

<b>Stay motivated</b>
• Earn XP, level up and unlock 12 achievements
• A little plant that grows as you level up, and droops if you disappear for a while
• Confetti. Lots of confetti.

<b>Home screen widgets</b>
Next task, focus timer with controls, today's progress, top tasks, habits, mood, quick capture and your level.

<b>Private by design</b>
No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone, with export and Android backup so you never lose it.
